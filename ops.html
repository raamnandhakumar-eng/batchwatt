How to read this evidence

Reduction = (baseline − with BatchWatt) / baseline × 100. Planning time is the cycle average. Energy uses aggregate kWh from the pilot records. Peak compares maximum concurrent equipment demand.

Estimated: energy outcomes are modeled from production and operational records. Recorded: planning time and peak values come from supplied workbooks. Independent meter verification is not attached.

Recommendations included moving high-load equipment, grouping compatible products, reducing changeover or idle time, and prioritizing near-due orders. Records include accepted, adjusted, executed and reviewed-only responses.

Source attachments, timestamped run logs and company confirmation remain pending in the pilot summaries. The sample is illustrative, not a replay of these pilots. Current planner features are separate from historical results.

Read methodology and evidence status

These are supplied pilot results from operational records / pilot workbooks. They are preserved as historical evidence and must not be retroactively attributed to newer BatchWatt features.
