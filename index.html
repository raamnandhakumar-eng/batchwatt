The product

One planning layer across dispatch, production, and energy.

BatchWatt does not replace a factory's current workflow. It reads the order book and stock sheet already in use, then produces an actionable daily plan and a traceable explanation.

01

Order and stock intake

Normalize WhatsApp orders and spreadsheet records while flagging missing quantities, ambiguous lines, and stale inventory.

02

Dispatch-risk detection

Compare due dates, finished-goods stock, shortages, and line availability before an order becomes a failed dispatch.

03

Production sequencing

Prioritize near-due orders, combine compatible SKU families, reserve material, and reduce avoidable cleaning or idle time.

04

Energy-aware planning

Compare baseline and optimized kWh and peak demand, while keeping modeled energy estimates distinct from metered savings.
