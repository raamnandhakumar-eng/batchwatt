**BatchWatt — Energy / operations decision-support platform | 2026**

- Built and deployed a Vercel decision-support tool that unified fragmented order, stock, production, and energy data from Excel/CSV and pasted orders into a single decision-support view; calculates shortages, ranks dispatch risk, recommends sequencing, and surfaces planning-time, energy, and peak-load indicators.

## Locked supporting facts

- Two operational factory pilots in 2026: **RKG Ghee** and **PR Food Products**.
- Pilot inputs included real order, stock, production, and energy data from the pilot environments.
- The operating workflow mapped information that had been handled through WhatsApp, Excel, phone calls, and memory into a dispatch-first planning product.
- Across both pilots: **19 planning cycles**, **73 orders**, **16 at-risk orders/dispatches**, and **14 recorded production-sequencing changes**.
