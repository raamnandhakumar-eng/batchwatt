# BatchWatt Pilot Methodology

## Purpose

This document defines how BatchWatt pilot results are calculated and what evidence is required before making public claims.

## Pilot workflow

Each planning cycle follows the same operating sequence:

1. Consolidate customer orders and due dates.
2. Review finished-goods stock, material availability, and packaging shortages.
3. Identify orders or dispatches at risk.
4. Generate a production sequence across the available lines or machines.
5. Review the recommendation with the factory team.
6. Record whether the recommendation was accepted, adjusted, executed, or reviewed only.
7. Compare planning time, estimated energy, and peak load against the baseline process.

## Metric definitions

### Planning-time reduction

Calculated from the average manual planning time before BatchWatt and the average planning time with BatchWatt:

`(baseline minutes - BatchWatt minutes) / baseline minutes`

### Orders flagged at risk

Count recorded in the daily pilot metrics for orders or dispatches surfaced before the planned due date or line start.

### Production sequencing changes

Count of planning cycles in which the factory team changed the baseline production order after reviewing the BatchWatt recommendation.

### Estimated energy reduction

Calculated from baseline and optimized kWh values in the daily pilot records. These values are modeled from the production sequence and operational records unless independently verified with meter data.

### Peak-load reduction

Calculated from the maximum baseline and optimized concurrent equipment demand recorded in the pilot dataset.

### Operator rating

Average 1–5 usability rating recorded across the pilot planning cycles.

## Evidence standard

The supplied workbook contains anonymized operational tables, daily metrics, production recommendations, and factory-team responses. Before describing the pilots as externally verified or customer-approved, retain:

- the original anonymized order source;
- the dated stock and production source sheets;
- timestamped BatchWatt run logs;
- screenshots showing inputs, recommendations, and results;
- a written company confirmation of the pilot period and workflow;
- written permission for any public company name or feedback quote.

## Safe public wording

Until the supporting evidence is attached and approved:

> BatchWatt contains operational pilot records for RKG Ghee and PR Food Products using order, stock, production, and energy data. Results shown are calculated from the supplied pilot workbook; external verification and publication permissions are tracked separately.

Do not describe the results as independently audited, customer-approved, or a fully integrated production deployment without the corresponding evidence.
